MARBLE HITTER

Marble Hitter is a fun and challenging physics-based game where you drag and launch a main marble to hit other marbles inside a specific area. Among the 10 marbles, 1 or 2 are of a different color — your goal is to knock those special marbles out of the area to win. But be careful! If any normal marble goes out, the game is over. It’s all about precision, timing, and strategy.

Treze Lines is a precision puzzle game where you draw paths to guide a ball from its start position to a target bowl. Each level introduces new obstacles and mechanics, challenging players to plan the perfect line. Simple to learn, with progressively tricky puzzles that reward creativity and timing.
